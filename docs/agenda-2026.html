Data Day 2026 AgendaPeople + Data = Inspired Futures

  • Tuesday, February 10, 2026
  • 8:00 a.m. – 4:00 p.m.
  • Center for Workforce Development, Columbus State Community College, 315 Cleveland Ave, Columbus, OH 43215

8:30 a.m. – 9:30 a.m. · Morning Plenary Session

Room 400 Ballroom
Changing the Way We Work: How Today’s C-Level Leaders Are Redefining Innovation Through Data and AI
Keynote: Salema Rice, CEO, IntelliCon Global

9:45 a.m. – 10:45 a.m. · Breakout Sessions

Room 400 Ballroom · Technical skill level ○○○

Sharing the Data of Lived Experience in “United: the Hidden Story of the Humble Duplex”

Join the Affordable Housing Alliance for a screening of “United: The Hidden Story of the Humble Duplex”. This film explores how the duplex maintained its role as a symbol of middle-class growth and mobility throughout Columbus. See how filmmaking was used to expand community education and humanize complex data.

Speaker: Carlie Boos, Affordable Housing Alliance of Central Ohio

Room 412 · Technical skill level ●●○

From Data to Action: The Central Ohio Climate Action Plan

Discover the Central Ohio Climate Action Plan, the region’s first roadmap for a sustainable future. Learn how greenhouse gas inventories, targets, and sector-based mitigation strategies help guide Central Ohio communities toward a more resilient, low-carbon future.

Speakers: Brian Filiatraut, MORPC; Roberto Burga, Sustainable Columbus

Room 414 · Technical skill level ●●○

Streamlining Work Planning for Maintenance Workforces

This session helps maintenance managers, GIS professionals, and executive staff better manage maintenance workforces. Learn about a complete solution featuring an integrated calendar, dashboards, reporting, and a web-based work management form that brings all tools together in one system.

Speaker: John Puente, Cultivate Geospatial Solutions

Room 420 · Technical skill level ○○○

What Happens When the Web Goes Dark

On October 20, 2025, a major data center outage disrupted essential apps across the eastern US, including Delaware County Transit’s dispatch system. Executive Director Andy Volenik shares how DCT adapted using proven processes and data-driven decision-making.

Speaker: Andy Volenik, Delaware County Transit

11:00 a.m. – 12:00 p.m. · Breakout Sessions

Room 400 Ballroom · Technical skill level ○○○

Meeting Current and Future Human Needs in Central Ohio: Data and Modeling for Insights and Action

As Central Ohio’s population grows, so will its needs for food, shelter, employment, child and elder care, healthcare, and government services. This presentation showcases two efforts to understand current and future human needs. Regionomics will discuss 25-year projections of those needs, and Smart Columbus will discuss the Community Information Exchange.

Speakers: Dr. Ayaz Hyder, Smart Columbus; Samyak Shrestha, Smart Columbus; Bill LaFayette, Ph.D., Regionomics

Room 412 · Technical skill level ●●○

Eviction Data Response Network of Franklin County

An update from a previous Data Day event most popular breakout session. Learn how the Franklin County Eviction Data Response Network uses interactive dashboards to visualize eviction filings, demographic disparities, and geospatial patterns, along with support legal reform, housing stability, and equitable, data-driven decision-making.

Speakers: Karyn Kerdolff, Center for Urban and Regional Analysis at The Ohio State University; Mindy Yocum, JusticeTech at The Ohio State University; Felix Engelmann, Computer Science and Engineering at The Ohio State University

Room 414 · Technical skill level ●●○

From Data to Decision: Lifecycle of an Economic Development Project

Ever wonder how companies choose locations for major investments? Learn how economic development professionals use data, including ArcGIS, Tableau, U.S. Census data, and business databases, to inform site selection. This interactive session shows how storytelling and statistics attract investment and talent to the region.

Speakers: Emily Canan, One Columbus; Allison Rowland, One Columbus; Tammy Palasigue, One Columbus

Room 420 · Technical skill level ●○○

Applications of Digital Twins in Industry

Digital Twin technology is transforming urban planning and business decisions. Explore real-world applications that simulate traffic, energy, and infrastructure systems before costly investments. Learn how digital twins support predictive analytics, resource optimization, and resilience, helping leaders shape strategies for mobility, sustainability, and economic growth.

Speaker: Nithin Santhanam, Honda Research Institute

12:30 p.m. – 1:30 p.m. · Lunch Plenary Session

Room 400 Ballroom
Panel Discussion
Moderator: Maria Schaper, MORPC
Panelists: Matthew Allison, Licking County Transit; Sophia Mohr, COTA; Stevie Pasamonte, Transit Columbus; Andy Volenik, Delaware County Transit

1:45 p.m. – 2:45 p.m. · Breakout Sessions

Room 400 Ballroom · Technical skill level ●●○

Responding to Community-Driven Resilience Needs Using Data

Learn how community co-design and data-driven strategies meet local needs despite federal funding cuts. Explore EPA, NOAA, and NFWF projects that use collaborative planning and GIS to drive resilience. This session is ideal for anyone looking to apply data skills to support and strengthen community resilience.

Speakers: Katie Kenney (Hasenstab), The Ohio State University; Heather Lochotzki, The Ohio State University; Kelsea Best, The Ohio State University; Lorrayne Miralha, The Ohio State University; Jim Stagge, The Ohio State University; Yvonka Hall, Northeast Ohio Community Resilience Centre

Room 412 · Technical skill level ○○○

Ohio Housing Needs Assessment: Using Data to Inform Housing Policy

This session highlights key findings from the FY26 Ohio Housing Needs Assessment, a biennial report by the Ohio Housing Finance Agency outlining the state’s housing challenges and strategic priorities. Learn how to access and use this data to inform policy, guide community impact, and support your own housing-related work.

Speaker: Devin Keithley, Ohio Housing Finance Agency

Room 414 · Technical skill level ●○○

Using Consumer Credit Data to Understand our Neighborhoods and Region

Regional economists have used credit history data for over a decade to study neighborhoods and metro areas. This session explores how the Mid-Ohio region compares on migration, retention, and financial wellbeing, discusses the data’s insights and limitations, and collectively identifies key topics for estimation and publication in 2026.

Speaker: Stephan Whitaker, Federal Reserve Bank of Cleveland

Room 420 · Technical skill level ●○○

Ohio Cyber Collaboration (OC3) Brief

Learn how local governments can protect critical data from cyber and ransomware attacks. This session covers the NIST Cybersecurity Framework, data inventory, classification, vulnerability identification, and practical steps to prioritize and secure sensitive assets, with resources available specifically for Ohio LGEs.

Speaker: Mark Bell, State of Ohio, Adjutants General’s Department

3:00 p.m. – 4:00 p.m. · Breakout Sessions

Room 400 Ballroom · Technical skill level ●○○

Is Franklin County Still Affordable? A Cost-of-Living Index Data Project

Explore how Scioto Analysis uses public data to create a Franklin County cost-of-living index, modeled on federal Regional Price Parity analysis. This session examines affordability drivers compared to peer counties and offers strategies for selecting and analyzing public data to understand local variations in housing, goods, services, taxes, wages, and inflation.

Speakers: Rob Moore, Scioto Analysis; Jacob Strang, Scioto Analysis

Room 412 · Technical skill level ●○○

CLEAN: Advancing Air Quality and Justice Through Community Participatory Science

In 2023, MORPC and Franklin County Public Health launched the Community Led Enhanced Air Quality Network (CLEAN), deploying low-cost sensors across neighborhoods to gather hyper-local data. This session explores how CLEAN reveals air pollution disparities in marginalized communities, identifies smaller-scale pollution sources, and assesses their impact on local air quality.

Speaker: JR Diaz-Hernandez, MORPC

Room 414 · Technical skill level ●●○

Data That Drives Change: Building Thriving, Measurable Communities

Data doesn’t just measure impact, it drives it. This session shows how data turned a powerful idea into a replicable community model. Learn to foster a culture of measurement, make confident data-driven decisions, and transform outcomes into compelling stories that inspire investment, equity, and community growth.

Speakers: Sheri Chaney Jones, Measurement Resources Company & SureImpact; Amy Klaben, Families Flourish

Room 420 · Technical skill level ●●○

Data Modernization in Action: Connecting People, Processes, and Platforms

Local governments have more data than ever but often lack a clear strategy, creating disconnected systems and static maps. Learn how planning, automation, and visualization can turn complexity into clarity and make GIS a dynamic tool. See how the MORPC Get Ready Initiative uses dashboards, Hub Sites, and StoryMaps to track projects.

Speakers: Bryan O’Reilly, Burgess & Niple; Mitchell Tijerina, Burgess & Niple
