About Data DayConnecting Central Ohio's data community

5th Annual Data Day - A one-day conference on better use of data, for local government, nonprofits, education, and civic-minded business across Central Ohio.

Five years in, the impact is clear.

Data Day has convened Central Ohio's data community around one belief: better use of data improves the region. In year five, we read the trend line and chart what's next.

What it is

A single day of breakout sessions across skill levels and topics, from data governance and analysis to open data, mapping, and responsible AI in the public sector, plus morning and lunch keynotes.

Who attends

Data users, practitioners, analysts, and decision-makers from local government, nonprofits, education, and civic-minded business.

Who convenes it

The Mid-Ohio Regional Planning Commission (MORPC) and its Regional Data Roundtable, serving 95+ local governments across a 15-county region.
