Mid-Ohio Regional Planning Commission.

The Mid-Ohio Regional Planning Commission (MORPC) is the regional council for Central Ohio, made up of more than 95 local governments across a 15-county region. MORPC is the regional voice, trusted convener, and catalyst, bringing Central Ohio communities together to collaborate on best practices and plan for the future growth and sustainability of the region.

Beyond its core role in regional transportation planning, MORPC offers expertise in research and forecasting, public policy, and funding, and convenes member governments, nonprofits, educational institutions, and civic-minded businesses around shared regional priorities.

Regional Data Roundtable.

The Regional Data Roundtable (RDR) is a top-level advisory roundtable of data experts from the public, private, nonprofit, and academic sectors. Members evaluate and advise the Commission and MORPC staff on matters of regional importance pertaining to data, and advance data policies and strategic actions that increase access to open data in Central Ohio.

The RDR's vision is to make Central Ohio a leader in using data to improve the quality of life for all residents. It hosts Data Day each year and supports ongoing working groups on cybersecurity, data, and information sharing.

Regional Data Agenda.

Every three years, the RDR defines the opportunity space for advancing the use of data and analytics in Central Ohio through the Regional Data Agenda. Adopted by the Commission in August 2026, the 2026–2028 agenda guides collaborative data, research, and analysis efforts across the region.

Four focus areas

Transportation & Mobility

Safe, efficient, and cost-effective transportation infrastructure and mobility services.

Housing & Community Development

Housing availability and affordability, transit-oriented development, and more walkable neighborhoods.

Infrastructure & Economic Development

Workforce housing, quality jobs, and more affordable childcare.

Energy & Sustainability

Protecting the environment, conserving natural resources, and expanding sustainable energy generation.

Four action pillars

Promote Reliable & Trusted Data

Build trust in data and systems to engender greater data use and investment.

Expand Education & Outreach

Foster advancements in data practices and public information. Data Day is this pillar's signature event.

Bolster Project & Program Support

Help scale or sustain new and novel data initiatives.

Increase Representation & Connection

Expand data access, further data storytelling, and increase community representation.
