Why present at Data Day.

Data Day is Central Ohio's annual conference for the public-sector data community, and 2027 is its fifth year. Each year it draws 200+ people from 75+ organizations across local governments, nonprofits, educational institutions, and civic-minded businesses. A 45-minute session puts your project, method, tool, or lesson learned in front of the practitioners and decision-makers who can use it.

Your work doesn't have to be finished

Lessons learned, a method that saved time, or a project that went sideways all make strong sessions.

The room is your peers

Attendees do the same kind of work across the region, so practical detail is welcome.

Any level works

Sessions range from "no technical background needed" to advanced.

The form is short

A few fields about your session. See exactly what we ask below.

What we're looking for.

All topics, every skill level

Every data topic is welcome. This year we especially encourage proposals on data governance, data management, data analysis, and data infrastructure.

Interaction and engagement

Strong proposals are practical and interactive. Tell us your takeaways for attendees and how you'll engage the room.

45 minutes, with Q&A

Each breakout runs 45 minutes, with significant time for questions and discussion. Individuals and teams are welcome.

What the proposal form asks

  • A session title
  • Up to three objectives: what attendees will learn or be able to do
  • A 100-word description for the website and program
  • A 300-word abstract
  • Speakers and their affiliations
  • The technical skill level, from all audiences to advanced
  • Up to two topic tags, shown in the agenda so attendees can find your session
  • How you'll engage the audience
