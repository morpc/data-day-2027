Put your organization in front of Central Ohio's data community

Data Day is the annual convening of the Regional Data Roundtable (RDR) at MORPC, which serves more than 95 local governments across a 15-county region. Attendees learn how Central Ohio uses, secures, governs, and visualizes data. Year five is a milestone, and a strong stage for your organization alongside a trusted MORPC signature event.
