**THE NEW THING**
- Fifth annual Data Day, this February.
- New way to get involved: **Data Day Champions**.
- Champions help at a few specific moments in the year.
- No committee. No recurring meetings. No year-long commitment.
- Five ways we are asking for your help, you pick what works for you.

**THE FIVE** (one line each, don't linger)
1. **Keynote connections** — early fall. Surface a name and why.
2. **Share the CFP** — late Sept into Oct. Forward it, nudge the person you know.
3. **Sponsor intros** — fall. Introductions only.
4. **Encourage registration** — winter, once it opens. Your team, your peers.
5. **Day-of role** — facilitating is the main one. Also greeting, room hosting, workshops.

- Most take an hour or two. Pick One or do all five.

**THE GUARDRAIL** (say this clearly, it's what sells it)
- Champions connect and amplify. MORPC makes decisions, handles nitty-gritty.
- Never on the hook for a deadline, a contract, or a conversation you don't want to have.
- No ask without the materials. CFP ask comes with copy, graphic, dates.

**THE EMAIL**
- Next day or two. Sign-up form, one-pager on the five ways, FAQ.
- Keynote already in mind? Separate form, no sign-up needed.

**CLOSE**
- Questions, grab me after or email me.
- We are excited about the Fifth annual Data Day. 
- Help us plot what comes next for Central Ohio's best data community.
- We'd appreciate your help. Thanks.
