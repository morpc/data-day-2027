## Why this list exists

The broad RIDG call went to 205 contacts and returned **7 sign-ups (3.4%)**. It also missed
almost everyone who built the 2026 program. Of the 35 people who spoke in 2026, only 6 are on
the roster at all: 3 signed up and 3 never replied. The other **29 were never contacted**. The
people most invested in Data Day have not been asked directly.

Use the [direct recruitment email](direct_recruitment_email.md). Each person below has a
ready-to-paste hook for its `[past session on X / leadership in Y / work on Z]` slot.

## Two rules that shape this list

**1. Sponsorship asks come first.** Anyone who is a live sponsorship contact is approached
about sponsoring before they are approached about being a Champion. The Champion ask is the
fallback after they decline. Sponsorship contacts route to Amanda McEldowney; see
[sponsor-intro-template.md](../Engagements/sponsor-intro-template.md).

**2. A 2026 decline counts as spent.** Anyone whose organization declined 2026 sponsorship
is free to receive a Champion ask now, without waiting on a 2027 sponsorship answer.

**Past facilitators are not on this list.** Every facilitator in the 2023-2026 programs was
MORPC staff, so facilitation has never been a volunteer role. Champion facilitators are new
in 2027, which is why day-of has no warm pool behind it.

## Before sending: the diversity check

Every name above comes from the set of people who have already presented at Data Day. That is
the fastest-converting pool and also the one that most reproduces the existing network, which
cuts directly against the representation pillar and the Phase 3 warning in the checklist.

Two concrete gaps in the current roster:

- **City of Columbus:** 25 people contacted, zero sign-ups. The region's largest local
  government is unrepresented among Champions.
- **Attendees who never presented.** The consolidated attendee lists in `Archive/` are a
  larger and more varied pool than the speaker set, and nobody has been recruited from them.

Add at least a few names from outside the past-speaker set to this wave rather than running it
purely off the program history.

## Tracking

Log every send in `champions-roster.csv`: set `emailtype` to `Direct`, fill `emaildate`, and
record the outcome in `responsedate` and the five ask columns. The broad call is currently
logged as `Broad (RIDG)` for 204 of 205 rows, so keeping the direct sends distinct is what
will show whether personal asks convert better than the 3.4% the broad call returned.
