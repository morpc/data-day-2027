## How this works

Same three steps as the keynote ask, and the same boundary: you open the door, staff walk
through it.

1. **Check first.** Ask your contact whether they are open to hearing about it.
2. **Introduce them** to Amanda McEldowney, who owns sponsorship, and pass along the
   brochure if it helps.
3. **Step back.** Staff handle the package, the pricing, the ask, and the invoicing.

**You never quote a price, negotiate, or make the ask.** If someone wants numbers, send
them the brochure or hand them to staff. Both are easier than fielding it yourself.

**If your own employer might sponsor,** that is welcome and common. Just hand it to staff
rather than running it yourself, which keeps you clear of any conflict of interest.

**Before you reach out,** check with Amanda. We keep a shared contact log so two people
are not approaching the same organization at once.

## The one-line version

> Data Day is MORPC's annual conference for Central Ohio's public-sector data community,
> 200+ attendees on February 18, 2027, in its fifth year. Sponsorships run from $500 to
> $5,000 across four levels. Every level includes an exhibitor table, logo placement on
> event materials, and event registrations. Higher levels add student scholarships, a
> breakout session to moderate or host, and speaking time in the plenary sessions.

Tiers are **Platinum $5,000, Gold $3,000, Silver $1,500, and Bronze $500.** The brochure
has the full benefit grid. Share that rather than describing benefits from memory.

## Step 1: Check that they are open

> **Subject:** Data Day 2027 sponsorship, worth a look?
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> I help out as a Champion for Data Day, MORPC's annual conference for Central Ohio's data
> community. The fifth one is February 18, 2027, and it draws 200+ people from local
> governments, nonprofits, universities, and civic-minded businesses.
>
> They are lining up sponsors now, and [organization] came to mind because [reason: your
> audience is in that room, you have sponsored similar events, your work touches this
> community]. Levels start at $500.
>
> Would you be open to an introduction to Amanda McEldowney, who handles sponsorship?
> Happy to forward the brochure either way.
>
> [Your name]

> sponsorship levels start at $500. Brochure attached. Amanda McEldowney

confirmed in **January 2027**, and anything arriving after that is hard to fit into the
printed program.
