**Subject:** Thank you for being a Data Day Champion! Got a keynote idea?

Hello Data Day Champion,

Thank you for signing up to be a Data Day Champion! Data Day 2027 is our fifth annual gathering of Central Ohio's data community. It takes place **Thursday, February 18, 2027**, at the **Center for Workforce Development at Columbus State Community College**. We're excited to have your help making the anniversary year a great one.

Your first chance to help is small. We are asking that you suggest an inspiring keynote speaker, a leader, practitioner, or storyteller doing interesting work with public-sector data. For more information about what makes a good keynote see the attached PDF.

**Simply fill out this keynote suggestion form** <https://forms.cloud.microsoft/r/mYmz1bctPH>

- Takes about 3 minutes
- Suggestions are open through **Friday, October 31**
- You just share the name and why they'd be great. MORPC staff take care of the invitation and follow-up.

Have more than one idea? Send as many as you like. 

Thanks again for being part of Data Day 2027!

Best,
Jordan Inskeep
Mid-Ohio Regional Planning Commission
