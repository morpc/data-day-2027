**Subject:** Data Day Champions: help us fill the program

Hello Data Day Champion,

Thank you again for being part of Data Day 2027! The **Call for Proposals is now open**. Proposals are due **Monday, November 30**.

The ask is small:

- **Share the call** through your channels: LinkedIn, email, a newsletter, or a team meeting.
- **Nudge two or three people** whose work you'd want to hear about. A personal note from you is the best way to bring in a strong session.

Everything you need is attached: the promo kit (ready-to-use posts, a "why present" blurb, and message templates), a promo slide with a QR code, and a social graphic.

**Submission form:** <https://tinyurl.com/dataday-cfp>

One timing note: the deadline is the Monday after Thanksgiving, so nudges sent by **Friday, November 20** have the best chance of landing. MORPC staff review every proposal and make the selections, so once you've shared or nudged, your part is done.

Thanks for helping make the fifth Data Day a great one!

Best,
Jordan Inskeep
Mid-Ohio Regional Planning Commission
