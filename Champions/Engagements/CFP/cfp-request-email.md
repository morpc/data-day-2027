**Subject:** Data Day Champions: help us fill the breakout program

Hello Data Day Champion,

Thank you for being part of Data Day 2027! The **Call for Proposals is open**, and your next way to help is getting great breakout sessions onto the program. Proposals are due **Monday, November 30**.

**The ask (do one or both):**

- **Share the call** on LinkedIn, by email, in a newsletter, or at a team meeting.
- **Nudge two or three people** whose work you'd want to hear about. A personal note from you is the best way to bring in a strong session.

**What's attached:**

- **Promo kit (PDF):** ready-to-post copy, a short "why present" blurb, and nudge templates you can adapt.
- **Promo slide (PDF and PNG):** for a meeting or presentation. It includes a QR code to the form.
- **Social card (PNG):** attach it to your post.

**Links to share:**

- Submit a proposal: <https://tinyurl.com/dataday-cfp>
- All the details on presenting: <https://morpc.github.io/data-day-2027/breakouts.html>

**Timing:** the deadline is the Monday after Thanksgiving, so nudges sent by **Friday, November 20** have the best chance of landing.

MORPC staff review every proposal and make the selections, so please don't promise anyone a slot. Once you've shared or nudged, your part is done.

Questions about whether an idea fits, or anything else? Just reply to this email.

Thanks for helping make the fifth Data Day a great one!

Best,
Jordan Inskeep
Mid-Ohio Regional Planning Commission
