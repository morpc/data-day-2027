Why present at Data Day

"Why present" blurb

Present at Data Day 2027. Data Day is Central Ohio's annual conference for the public-sector data community, and 2027 is its fifth year. Each year it draws 200+ people from 75+ organizations across local governments, nonprofits, educational institutions, and civic-minded businesses. A 45-minute session puts your project, method, tool, or lesson learned in front of the practitioners and decision-makers who can use it. Every data topic and every skill level is welcome, and this year the program especially encourages proposals on data governance, data management, data analysis, and data infrastructure. Proposals are due Monday, November 30, 2026.

One-line version

Doing data work the region should hear about? Data Day 2027 wants your session proposal by November 30: https://forms.cloud.microsoft/r/p5reURxZpZ

Reasons that land with hesitant presenters

  • Your work doesn't have to be finished or flashy. Lessons learned, a method that saved time, or a project that went sideways all make strong sessions.
  • The room is your peers. Attendees do the same kind of work across the region, so practical detail is welcome.
  • Any level works. Sessions range from "no technical background needed" to advanced.
  • The form is short. A title, up to three objectives, a 100-word description, a 300-word abstract, speakers, skill level, up to two topic tags, and how you'll engage the room.
