# Data Day 2027 Champions: Keynote Introduction Templates

> **Audience:** champion-facing. Sent to a Champion after they indicate on the
> [suggestion form](https://forms.cloud.microsoft/r/mYmz1bctPH) that they would make a
> warm introduction, and after staff have reviewed the name.
> **Status:** final. Pairs with
> [keynote-criteria-one-pager.md](keynote-criteria-one-pager.md).

---

## How this works

Three short steps. You only ever write to someone you already know.

1. **Check first.** Ask your contact whether they are open to an introduction. Never
   forward their details to us before they say yes.
2. **Introduce us.** Connect them to Jordan Inskeep by email.
3. **Step back.** MORPC staff take the invitation from there. You are welcome to stay on
   the thread, but nothing further is asked of you.

**Please do not** discuss honoraria, travel, or dates, promise a slot, or mention the
candidate publicly. Those are staff decisions and staff announcements.

---

## Step 1: Check that they are open

Adapt freely. Your own voice works better than ours.

> **Subject:** A quick question about Data Day 2027
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> I help out as a Champion for Data Day, the annual conference MORPC runs for Central
> Ohio's data community. It is February 18, 2027, and it will be the fifth year. They are
> looking for keynote speakers and you were the first person I thought of, given your work
> on [specific thing].
>
> The theme is "Plotting What's Next," so they are after a forward look at where
> public-sector data work is heading rather than a retrospective. Roughly 200 people from
> local governments, nonprofits, universities, and civic-minded businesses.
>
> Would you be open to an introduction to Jordan Inskeep, who runs the event? No
> commitment at all, just a conversation to see whether it fits.
>
> [Your name]

**If they say no,** that is a perfectly good outcome. Let us know and we will close the
loop on our end. No follow-up needed.

---

## Step 2: Make the introduction

Send once they have agreed. Copy Jordan at jinskeep@morpc.org.

> **Subject:** Intro: [Candidate name] <> Jordan Inskeep (Data Day 2027)
>
> Jordan, meet [Name], [role] at [organization]. [One or two sentences on their work and
> why the room would want to hear from them.]
>
> [Name], Jordan Inskeep leads Data Day for MORPC. Data Day is the region's annual
> gathering for the public-sector data community, February 18, 2027, in its fifth year.
> Jordan can share the theme, the format, and what a keynote involves.
>
> I will let the two of you take it from here.
>
> [Your name]

## Short variants

**LinkedIn message:**

> Hi [Name], MORPC is scouting keynote speakers for Data Day 2027 (February 18, Columbus),
> their fifth annual conference for Central Ohio's data community. The theme is a forward
> look at where public-sector data work is heading, and your work on [thing] fits it well.
> Open to an intro to the organizer? No commitment.

**In person or by phone:**

> MORPC runs Data Day every February for the regional data community, and they are looking
> for keynotes for the fifth one. You came to mind because of [thing]. Want me to connect
> you with the organizer? It is just a conversation.

---

## If they ask something you cannot answer

Common ones: fees, travel, how long the talk is, whether they can bring slides, who else
is speaking. The answer is the same for all of them.

> That is a better question for Jordan. Let me connect you.
