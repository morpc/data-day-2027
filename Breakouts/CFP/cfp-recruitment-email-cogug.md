**Subject:** GIS users: present at Data Day 2027 (proposals due Nov 30)

Hello COGUG members,

Data Day, Central Ohio's annual convening of public-sector data users, practitioners, and analysts, is marking its **fifth year** on Thursday, February 18, 2027. The Regional Data Roundtable is seeking breakout session proposals, and GIS practitioners have a lot to offer this year's program.

This year's priority topics are **data governance, data management, data analysis, and data infrastructure**. That's the foundation of good GIS work. Consider sharing:

- How your organization governs, maintains, and shares spatial data
- Data quality, standards, or stewardship practices that keep your layers reliable
- The infrastructure behind your maps: databases, pipelines, portals, and integrations
- Spatial analysis that informed a real decision in your community

**Event details:**

- **When:** Thursday, February 18, 2027
- **Where:** Center for Workforce Development, Columbus State Community College
- **Format:** 45-minute sessions with plenty of time for Q&A and discussion
- **Deadline:** Monday, November 30, 2026

**Submit a proposal:** <https://tinyurl.com/dataday-cfp>
**Details on presenting:** <https://morpc.github.io/data-day-2027/breakouts.html>

Strong proposals are practical and interactive, with clear takeaways and a plan for engaging the room. Have a colleague whose work belongs on the program? Please pass this along.

Thank you,
