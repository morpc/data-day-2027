**Subject:** Present at Data Day 2027: proposals due November 30

Hello,

The Regional Data Roundtable invites you to help shape the program for the **fifth annual Data Day**, Central Ohio's convening of public-sector data users, practitioners, and analysts.

We're looking for breakout sessions that share the tools, analyses, projects, and lessons moving data work forward in the region. This year we especially encourage proposals on **data governance, data management, data analysis, and data infrastructure**. Every data topic is welcome.

- **When:** Thursday, February 18, 2027
- **Where:** Center for Workforce Development, Columbus State Community College
- **Format:** 45-minute sessions with plenty of time for Q&A and discussion
- **Deadline:** Monday, November 30, 2026

**Submit a proposal:** <https://tinyurl.com/dataday-cfp>
**Details on presenting:** <https://morpc.github.io/data-day-2027/breakouts.html>

Strong proposals are practical and interactive, with clear takeaways and a plan for engaging the room. Local governments, nonprofits, educational institutions, and civic-minded businesses are all encouraged to submit.

Know someone whose work should be on the program? Please forward this along.

Thank you,
Jordan Inskeep
Mid-Ohio Regional Planning Commission
