# Lunch Keynote Panel (Internal)

Working summary of the Data Day 2027 lunch panel. Internal/staff use only.

## Concept
- **Working description:** How to build multi-sector collaborative programs for public good, focusing on community-engaged research and data-driven assessment.
- **Theme tie-in:** *Plotting What's Next*. Highlights collaborative research and community programs with a strong data-centered practice, looking ahead at how data can drive positive change in Central Ohio.
- **Origin:** Expands on Amy and Sheri's 2026 breakout "Data That Drives Change" (Families Flourish and data-driven community investment), which was well received. Jason added for the engaged-research perspective.
- **Story arc:** Program evaluation moving from academic proof-of-concept (*does it work?*) to long-term practice (*how do we keep getting effective results?*), with Amy speaking for the program across both phases.
- **Background:** See `families_flourish_background.md` (local only, git-ignored; contains non-public details).
